## CV conventions: United States
- US résumé norms: one page (two only for senior/staff roles with 10+ relevant years).
- Contact line: city and state (or "Remote"), email, phone if the master CV has one, LinkedIn/GitHub/portfolio.
- Never include a photo, date of birth, age, gender, marital status or nationality (US anti-discrimination norms).
- State work authorization (e.g. "Authorized to work in the US, no sponsorship needed") only if the candidate profile gives it.
