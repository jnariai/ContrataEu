## CV conventions: International / EU (remote and European jobs)
- Call it a CV. One page; two only for 10+ years of directly relevant experience.
- Contact line: city and country (or "Remote"), email, phone if the master CV has one, LinkedIn/GitHub/portfolio.
- No photo, date of birth, nationality or marital status. Mention work authorization (e.g. EU citizen, visa needs) only if the candidate profile gives it and the job is location-bound.
