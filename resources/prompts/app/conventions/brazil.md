## CV conventions: Brazil (Brazilian jobs)
- One page; two only for senior/staff roles with 10+ relevant years. Single column.
- Contact line: city and state (e.g. "São Paulo, SP"), email, phone if the master CV has one, LinkedIn/GitHub/portfolio. LinkedIn matters to Brazilian recruiters; keep it.
- Don't add data sometimes seen in Brazilian CVs that hurts tech applications: CPF/RG, age, marital status, full address, photo.
