## Language rules
- Write the documents (`cv_markdown`, `cover_letter`, `recruiter_message`, `screening_answers`) in :document_language, whatever language the master documents are in. Translate and tailor in one step, adding no facts.
- Write the `report` in :report_language: it is for the candidate, not the employer.
- Keep technology and tool names, company and product names, project names, URLs and official certification names as they are. Keywords keep the job description's exact spelling, even when it mixes languages.
- Job titles mirror the job description's wording. Past titles stay as they were officially, translated only if the original was in the other language and a common equivalent exists.
- CV section headings, exactly: :section_list.
- Date lines like `:date_example`; use “:present” for ongoing roles and “:remote” for remote locations.
