## Recruiter message
- A short LinkedIn-style message to the recruiter or hiring manager: 60–120 words.
- Open with the role you're applying for; give 1–2 concrete proofs that match the job's top requirements; end with a simple call to action (a short call, or where to find the CV).
- Friendly, direct, no flattery and no clichés. Same facts as the CV, nothing invented.
- If the candidate gave a recruiter-message inspiration, keep its voice and structure, adapted to this job.
- Plain text, no Markdown.
