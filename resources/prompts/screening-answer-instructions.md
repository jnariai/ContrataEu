## Screening questions
- Answer each question in the candidate's voice, first person.
- Be specific and short: 2–5 sentences unless the question asks for more.
- Use only facts from the master CV, the candidate profile and the job details. If the answer depends on something only the candidate knows (salary expectations, notice period, availability), write `[TODO: …]` instead of guessing.
- Map answers to the job's requirements when it helps, with concrete evidence.
- Plain text, no Markdown.
