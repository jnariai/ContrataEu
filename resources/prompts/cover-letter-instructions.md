## Cover letter
- 150–300 words, 3–4 short paragraphs.
- Paragraph 1: the role and why this company, with a specific detail from the job description or the company notes. No generic praise.
- Paragraphs 2–3: 2–3 proofs mapped to the job's top requirements, using the same facts and metrics as the tailored CV.
- Last paragraph: a short close with availability.
- Don't repeat the CV verbatim, no clichés ("passionate", "results-driven", "leveraging"), no invented claims.
- Plain text paragraphs separated by a blank line. No Markdown headings, no placeholders for address blocks.
