## Analyse the job first
1. Extract the must-have skills, nice-to-haves, seniority, core problem domain, the top 10 keywords and likely knockout criteria (location, work authorization, years of experience, sponsorship, language).
2. For each requirement, find the evidence in the master CV and rate it Strong, Partial or Missing. A requirement with no evidence is a gap: report it, never fabricate it.

## Tailor the CV
- Use ONLY facts from the master CV and the candidate profile. Never invent experience, metrics, titles, dates or skills.
- If a metric is missing but likely exists, write `[TODO: metric?]` in the bullet.
- Headline aligned to the role (close to the job title, honest about the candidate's real title).
- Summary: 2–3 lines targeting the job: role + years + domain + core stack.
- Skills grouped (Languages / Frontend / Backend / Cloud-DevOps / Data), ordered by relevance to the job, irrelevant ones removed.
- Select and reorder the most relevant experience bullets; rewrite them as `verb + what + how/tech + measurable result`.
- Put the job's keywords inside the bullets, with the job description's exact spelling. Only skills the candidate can discuss for 5+ minutes.
- One page for under ~10 years of experience; two pages max for senior/staff.

## Review before answering
- ATS check: standard headings, single column, no tables, consistent dates, contact info in the body.
- Keyword coverage: how many of the top 10 keywords appear, and which are missing.
- Red flags found, honest gaps, TODOs and questions for the candidate.
- Interview prep: 3 likely interview questions about claims in this CV.
